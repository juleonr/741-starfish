History

Provenance

Built by
Citation Homes

Your home was built by Citation Builders — a mid-sized Bay Area residential developer founded in 1977 by Stephen C. Schott and partners, operating out of San Leandro, California. Shadow Ridge Unit No. 1 (Westpointe) was one of their earliest major projects, filed just eight years after the company's founding.

In 1988, Schott bought out his partners and reformed the company as Citation Homes Central, relocating to Santa Clara. Despite a lean staff of fewer than 25 employees, the firm became one of California's largest homebuilders — responsible for over 50,000 homes statewide.

Stephen Schott was inducted into the California Homebuilding Foundation Hall of Fame in 2004. He is also widely known as the co-owner of the Oakland Athletics from 1995–2005 — the team immortalized in Michael Lewis's Moneyball.
